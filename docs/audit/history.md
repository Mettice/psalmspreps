## Risks and notes

- **Religion (rows 6, 24–28):** describe beliefs neutrally ('Christians believe…'); questions must test facts about history, not belief.
- **Row 10 'The pygmies in Cameroon':** the sheet's word is considered offensive by some; use the peoples' own names (Baka, Bagyeli, Bakola, Bedzang) and keep the sheet title only as a reference. Needs a teacher's decision.
- **Estimated dates** differ between sources; a wrong 'exact' date is a P1 error. Prefer 'about' and ranges.
- **Overlaps:** Egyptian and Indian numerals (Maths Lessons 1 and 3): reuse the reviewed wording (TR-D01).
