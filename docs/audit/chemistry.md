## Engine gaps

- **Matching**, **sort into groups**, **labelling**, **fact-table templates** (as Biology).
- **Units library** beyond length: mass (mg, g, kg), volume (cm³, mL, L, dm³), time, temperature. `convert` in `src/engine/lib/maths.js` only handles metric length today.
- **Instrument renderers** (code-drawn SVG with the reading computed): measuring cylinder with meniscus, balance, thermometer, stopwatch, pH colour scale, pie chart.
- **Chemistry helpers**: count atoms in a simple formula; name a salt from acid + base; classify pH.
- **Subscripts** in rendered text (H₂O): Unicode subscripts are enough, no MathML needed.

## Risks and notes

- **Safety rows (5, 6)** are P1: a wrong hazard meaning could hurt someone.
- **Scope:** some titles (gas-gas separation, solutions, solubility) may go beyond what Form 1 teaches in practice; the teacher should confirm depth before writing.
- **Overlaps:** water and pollution (Biology), water cycle (Geography), lab equipment and hazard symbols (Physics), states of matter (Physics rows 11–12). Write once, share.
