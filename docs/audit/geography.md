## Engine gaps

- **Geography library**: local time from longitude (and the reverse), latitude/longitude formatting (°N/S/E/W), 4- and 6-figure grid references, map scale (cm on map → km).
- **Code-drawn maps**: a grid map and a graticule drawn from the question's own numbers, so the answer is computed; a sample map with marginal information.
- **Climograph renderer** (rainfall bars + temperature line) and helpers: range, total, wettest month.
- **Drawn SVG maps** (world, Cameroon natural regions), simplified to stay small.
- **Labelling**, **matching**, **sort into groups**, **fact-table templates**.

## Review burden (P1)

Estimate: **~123 P1 items** = 110 from the rows (6 C × 2 + 9 F × 4 + 8 A × 7 + 2 X × 3; deferred rows count 0 for now) + 10 drawn diagrams or maps (labels must be right) + 3 conventions to settle first:

- Tropics and polar circles: 23.5° / 66.5° or 23°26′ / 66°34′.
- Climograph: the rule for a 'wet' or 'dry' month (a rainfall threshold, or P = 2T).
- Time: GMT or UTC in wording; Cameroon is UTC+1 (West Africa Time).

Reviewer: a Geography teacher. The Cameroon map also needs a check of the region boundaries.

## Risks and notes

- **Map accuracy:** the Cameroon natural-regions map is P1 and the most likely to be wrong if drawn by hand; trace it from a public-domain source and note the source.
- **Real station data** for climographs needs a source; generated data avoids that but should still look like a real Cameroonian place.
- **Overlaps:** atmosphere and water cycle (Chemistry), climate change (Physics, English), waste (Home Economics).
