# Form Five Geography — teacher review

- TR-geography-1: Row 4 mixes AICs and LICs in its title but asks about high industrialisation. The draft explicitly develops advanced industrial profiles; confirm the intended heading. No spine correction was made. School industrial categories are not equated to World Bank income groups.
- TR-geography-2: Confirm dated income terminology: the World Bank FY2027 table identifies Uganda as low income and Cameroon as lower-middle income. These are income categories, not a direct measurement of industrial structure. Avoid generalising constraints or capabilities to every person, firm or place.
- TR-geography-3: Confirm syllabus depth for industrial types, Uganda park examples, historical German industrial landscape and Cameroon examples. ALUCAM’s Edéa identification is sourced; present output, employment, inputs and performance are not asserted. Original local business cases are fictional.
- TR-geography-4: Weber practice compares only three listed triangle vertices with equal link distances and equal per-tonne-kilometre charges. It does NOT find an unrestricted spatial optimum; such an optimum can lie between vertices. Confirm the school’s treatment of transport, labour and agglomeration and the level of mathematical extension required.
- TR-geography-5: Practicals 7/21 are screen-based guided preparation with changing invented maps, criteria and distances. Supply authentic local land-use/topographic maps and supervised field evidence for full practical assessment. Classroom plot exclusions are not legal zoning rules. A marked hazard boundary is not a real flood survey.
- TR-geography-6: Road, rail, air, water, motorway, cableway and pipeline examples simplify mode choice. No current timetable, fare, travel time, speed limit or safety/route guarantee is supplied. Pipeline material supports the historical route to an offshore loading facility near Kribi; no commissioning date or current flow is assessed.
- TR-geography-7: The selected Cameroon diagram is original, incomplete and not to scale. It distinguishes selected Douala–Yaoundé–Ngaoundéré rail links from a Douala–Kribi road link. It does not assert railway service to Kribi or show every road, port or transport mode. Confirm desired local route and airport case-study depth.
- TR-geography-8: Repeated rows 17/19 address network media then meaningful access; repeated rows 18/20 address selected corridors then full multimodal journeys. Confirm this allocation against the teacher’s intended progression.
- TR-geography-9: Trade volume distinguishes physical tonnage, money values and constant-price volume indices. The numerical task explicitly defines combined physical flow, not a balance of trade. Confirm exam terminology and add real dated datasets with units and provenance.
- TR-geography-10: Bloc categories are simplified distinguishing-feature models, not a compulsory universal sequence. AfCFTA aims are distinguished from actual implementation. No current membership totals, tariff rates or travel entitlements are taught. Confirm dated case studies, CEMAC scope and accepted trade-creation/diversion depth.
- TR-geography-11: All invented practical outputs are automatically checkable model decisions. Technical tests and browser walks do not establish teacher approval, real field competence or realised development outcomes. Further-study row 13 is excluded by row policy, not silently completed.
