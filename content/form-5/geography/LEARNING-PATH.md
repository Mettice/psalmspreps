# Form Five Geography — learning path

Opening Learn screens give a measurable outcome, earlier knowledge and next use. Maps and datasets are original inventions unless explicitly identified as selected real nodes.

| Row | Topic | Outcome | Builds on | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manufacturing industries | classify an activity from its transformation of inputs into manufactured outputs | Form Four economic activities | industrial clusters and location |
| 2 | Agglomeration industries | explain a cluster benefit and a possible cost using evidence | manufacturing inputs and services | industrial development and location |
| 3 | Industrialisation in less industrialised economies | explain how a stated constraint can limit manufacturing and propose a relevant response | inputs and agglomeration | comparison with advanced industrial profiles |
| 4 | Advanced industrial profiles: capabilities and changing employment | explain why productivity and employment shares can change differently | constraints on industrialisation | location models and industrial change |
| 5 | Weber’s industrial location model | calculate transport effort for listed candidate sites and state the model’s limits | inputs, outputs and location factors | Cameroon industry and land-use choices |
| 6 | Secondary industries in Cameroon | connect a manufacturing activity to an input, location factor and possible impact | manufacturing and location models | guided land-use decisions |
| 7 | Practical: location of economic activities and land use | read a site map and justify a choice against every stated criterion | industrial location and competing land uses | communication networks and route decisions |
| 8 | Communication: information, transport and networks | distinguish information exchange from physical transport and identify network elements | location and access | road and rail development |
| 9 | Development of road transport | explain a road-development factor and calculate a model journey time | network links and access | rail transport and economic impacts |
| 10 | Development of rail transport | explain rail’s infrastructure requirements and compute freight tonne-kilometres | road transport and networks | comparison of transport impacts |
| 11 | Economic impacts of road and rail transport | explain a transport benefit and cost without assuming equal outcomes | road and rail development | air and water transport decisions |
| 12 | Air and water transport | choose a mode for a stated cargo and explain the trade-off | road and rail impacts | specialised transport and pipelines |
| 14 | Specialised transport: motorways and cableways | match specialised infrastructure to a stated need and limitation | transport modes and route constraints | pipeline networks |
| 15 | Pipelines | explain pipeline suitability and a related constraint | specialised fixed-route transport | global flows and telecommunications |
| 16 | Globalisation | trace a cross-border connection and explain an uneven effect | transport and communication systems | telecommunication networks and trade |
| 17 | Telecommunications: signals and networks | identify how a telecommunication channel connects users and state a limitation | communication and globalisation | Cameroon networks and digital access |
| 18 | Transport development in Cameroon: routes and nodes | read selected Cameroon transport links without inventing missing connections | road and rail networks | access inequalities and multimodal planning |
| 19 | Telecommunications: access and the digital divide | compare barriers to meaningful digital access and propose a matched response | signals and networks | Cameroon access planning and trade information |
| 20 | Transport development in Cameroon: connecting the full journey | evaluate a multimodal proposal using access, transfers and maintenance | Cameroon routes and access inequalities | map-network practicals |
| 21 | Practical: communication on maps | read a network legend and select a shortest open route with working | network links, transfers and constraints | trade flows and volume |
| 22 | Volume of trade | distinguish physical trade quantity from money value and calculate a comparable total | transport flows and network maps | regional trade arrangements |
| 23 | Trade blocs: forms and distinguishing features | classify an invented arrangement from its stated integration features | trade measures and cross-border connections | regional opportunities and limitations |
| 24 | Trade blocs: opportunities, implementation and limits | assess a regional trade proposal and calculate an invented within-region share | forms of trade integration | later trade case studies and development comparisons |

Twenty-one lesson rows and guided map practicals 7/21 are active drafts. Further-study row 13 (Containerization) is excluded by the authoring row policy. Repeated telecommunications and Cameroon transport rows develop distinct network/access and corridor/whole-chain topics.

Overall destination: explain how production, access, information and trade connect places; use a map or dataset to justify a choice and state its limits.
