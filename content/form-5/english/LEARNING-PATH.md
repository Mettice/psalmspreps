# Form Five English — learning path

Every opening Learn screen states its goal, builds-on knowledge and next use. Prerequisites record dependencies.

| Row | Topic | By the end | Builds on | Leads to |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Teaching rows 2–32 are included; row 1 is diagnostic/evaluation and excluded. Spine sequence governs overlapping date windows.

Overall destination: interpret evidence and produce purposeful English through controlled grammar, developed writing and clear audience choices. Written oral preparation requires separate live/audio practice.
