# Form Five English — teacher review

All Term One teaching rows 2–32 are active drafts. Row 1 remains diagnostic. These resources are not teacher-approved examination standards.

- TR-english-1: Confirm depth and pace, especially foundation rows 9, 10, 11, 22, 28 and 29 adapted from our Form Four resources.
- TR-english-2: Check local essay lengths, speech conventions, quotation punctuation, letter layout and greeting/closing requirements. Models illustrate purpose and development rather than a universal examination template.
- TR-english-3: Rows 3, 12, 17 and 28 prepare speaking in writing; rows 5, 18 and 30 use written listening scripts. Supply oral practice and assess hearing, pronunciation, fluency and interaction separately. Row 12 selects consonant pairs, not the whole inventory.
- TR-english-4: Scripts and articles are original classroom fiction. No prescribed text, real witness quotation, employment rule, business return or pollutant measurement is supplied. Resource discussions do not authorise physical intervention.
- TR-english-5: Writing tasks include original models and checklists. Check whether broad spine rows need further class-specific prompts and report accepted alternatives or omissions through teacher feedback.
