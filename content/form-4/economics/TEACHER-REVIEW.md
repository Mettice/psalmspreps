# Form Four Economics — Term One

Draft rows 1–20, each with visible end goal, earlier knowledge, next use, Learn, three cards, a worked example, five varied questions and specific wrong-option feedback. LEARNING-PATH.md records the sequence across the term; lesson JSON records earlier numbered prerequisites.

- [ ] **TR-economics-1 — Local legal terminology.** Confirm the ordinary sole-proprietor/general-partnership models and their relationship to actual OHADA forms. Company and cooperative frameworks were consulted, but these drafts do not give a registration checklist, owner-number limit, current legal minimum capital or tax rule. A single-owner incorporated company must not be confused with an unincorporated sole proprietor. Limited liability does not cancel separate guarantees or investment losses.
- [ ] **TR-economics-2 — Company capital.** Rows 6/7 explicitly use the school authorised/issued/called/paid-up instalment model. Confirm its expected examination terminology and distinguish nominal value, market value, debt, retained earnings, reserves and reserve capital. The inequalities and reconciliations concern that model, not every real legal capital arrangement. Dividend examples explicitly declare a distribution on identical shares; they are not forecasts.
- [ ] **TR-economics-3 — Guided rows.** Spine rows 5 and 11 are labelled lesson but titled directed/guided work. Included as fictional comparison and planning preparation, without real formation, registration, spending or household financial disclosure. Row 7 is a practical using a labelled capital record and computed table. Confirm the classroom/examination treatment of these rows.
- [ ] **TR-economics-4 — Cooperative rules.** Confirm the primary-cooperative voting example, service classifications and local statutory treatment. One member, one vote is not a claim that surplus payments must be equal. Patronage calculations use only the explicitly distributable amount and stated eligible transactions; no actual return is promised.
- [ ] **TR-economics-5 — Ownership policies.** Nationalisation, privatisation, regulation, commercialisation and liberalisation are distinguished. All transfers and enterprises are invented, so no current ownership, compensation, sale or performance of a real Cameroonian enterprise is asserted. Confirm the class text’s preferred definitions and local examples before adding names.
- [ ] **TR-economics-6 — Scale and growth.** Confirm examination depth on internal/external economies and diseconomies, integration and measures of firm size. Average-cost comparisons are explicitly comparable long-run plans, not an unsupported inference from total cost. Internal growth is a method, not a restriction to one funding source. Percentage growth uses the original value.
- [ ] **TR-economics-7 — Location and small firms.** Confirm terminology location/localisation and the breadth of expected examples. No universal size cutoff or live wage, fare, land cost or site recommendation is supplied. Site arithmetic compares stated items only; reliability, demand, social effects and omitted costs still require evidence.

Original cases use Cameroon settings, fictional CFA-franc amounts and computed answers. Validation tests and browser walks do not replace a Cameroon subject teacher’s approval of curriculum depth and marking conventions.
