# Literature in English — Form Four, Term One

Rows 2 and 19 are edition-independent foundations using original invented prose and scripts. Row 1 is diagnostic and excluded. Rows 3–18 and 20–38 await teacher feedback, as Dion requested on 2026-10-24.

1. **TR-literature-1 — Texts and editions.** The spine names The Crown of Thorns and Shakespeare’s Macbeth. Confirm author, edition, chapter/page mapping and the school’s required extracts before writing text-specific analysis. In particular, Macbeth’s ending is divided differently across editions: the Folger text presents the final events within its last scene, while RSC teaching separates a further final scene. Do not assume line/scene references are interchangeable. No prescribed-text lesson is claimed complete here.
2. **TR-literature-2 — Story analysis.** Confirm the school’s terminology for plot, setting, characterisation, theme and point of view. The foundation separates topic/summary from an interpretation linked to evidence. The short original passages cannot establish a whole novel’s theme or an author’s biography. More than one supported interpretation can be valid; exercise choices deliberately oppose supported claims to summary or overreach.
3. **TR-literature-3 — Dramatic convention.** Confirm treatment of dialogue, stage directions, aside, soliloquy and dramatic irony. Audience access differs from a character’s access. The original examples specify who can hear an aside. Soliloquies present private thought, but actual staging can vary; not every long speech is one, and a rule requiring an empty stage in every production is avoided.
4. **TR-literature-4 — Performance and assessment.** These are written analysis lessons, not recordings or acting/listening evaluations. Verify expected exam depth and provide actual performance practice if required. All fictional school settings/characters/scripts are newly authored; no prescribed passage is copied or replaced.
5. **TR-literature-5 — Feedback status.** Collect teacher decisions rather than asking pupils for texts or inventing replacements. Deferred rows are excluded from the playable lesson count and remain visible in docs/CODEX-CONTENT-STATUS.md. Technical validation is not teacher approval.
