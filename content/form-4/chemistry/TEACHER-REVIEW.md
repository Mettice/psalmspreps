# Form 4 Chemistry: points for the subject teacher (TR-K4-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Open items:

- **TR-K4-1 (all rows).** Formulae and equations are drawn by the engine (subscripts from `formula()`), so a pupil
  never sees a typed subscript. Coefficients are written in front, as usual.
- **TR-K4-2 (numbering).** The sheet's own lesson numbers do not match its row order: rows 10, 18 and 30 are
  integration activities, and several titles arrived from the PDF with a doubled prefix ("LesLesson 17"). The app
  numbers lessons by their place in the year (proposal G1, approved 2026-10-17), so the app's Lesson 19 is the
  sheet's Lesson 17. Please confirm this is acceptable, or give the numbering the class uses.
- **TR-K4-3 (all rows).** Relative atomic masses used: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, S 32, Cl 35.5, K 39,
  Ca 40, Fe 56, Cu 64, Zn 65, Pb 207. Molar volume at s.t.p. 22.4 dm³/mol; Avogadro constant 6.02 × 10²³.
- **TR-K4-4 (rows 1-9).** Sulphur: Frasch water at about 170 °C; transition temperature 96 °C; melting point
  about 115 °C; densities 2.07 and 1.98 g/cm³. Hydrogen sulphide is prepared with hydrochloric acid, never nitric.
  Sulphur dioxide is said to bleach by reduction (temporary) and chlorine by oxidation (permanent).
- **TR-K4-5 (rows 11-17).** Nitrogen: air taken as 78 per cent nitrogen by volume; ammonia dried with quicklime,
  never with concentrated sulphuric acid; nitric acid distilled in all-glass apparatus. Nitrates on heating follow
  the three patterns (Na/K → nitrite; most metals → oxide; Ag/Hg → metal). The nitrogen cycle names Rhizobium,
  Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter. Please confirm the depth and the names the class uses.
