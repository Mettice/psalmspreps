# Form 4 Physics: points for the subject teacher (TR-P4-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Open items:

- **TR-P4-1 (rows 3–5).** Constants used: specific heat capacity of water 4200, aluminium 900, copper 400, iron 460 J/(kg·K);
  specific latent heat of fusion of ice 336 000 J/kg; of vaporisation of water 2 260 000 J/kg. T(K) = θ(°C) + 273, as in
  Forms 1–3 (TR-P03). Please confirm the values the class uses.
- **TR-P4-2 (row 6).** The applications of conduction, convection and radiation are everyday Cameroonian examples
  (cooking pots, light clothing, a refrigerator, a solar water heater, room vents). Add or replace any the class prefers.
- **TR-P4-3 (row 7).** The bimetallic strip is taught as brass with invar, with brass on the outside when heated.
  Water is said to be densest at 4 °C. Radiant energy converters named: solar panel, solar water heater, green leaf.
- **TR-P4-4 (row 14).** Speed of sound taken as 340 m/s in air (a few questions use 330 or 350) and 1500 m/s in sea water;
  about 5000 m/s in steel. Audible range given as 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz.
- **TR-P4-5 (row 11).** Refraction is taught as: the frequency never changes at a boundary, so v and λ change together.
  Diffraction is said to be greatest when the gap is about one wavelength wide.
- **TR-P4-6 (rows 12, 15).** Stationary waves use λ = 2L/n, with the nth harmonic called the (n−1)th overtone.
  The string formula is given as f = (1/2L)√(T/µ), checked with a sonometer. Confirm the depth expected at Form 4.
- **TR-P4-7 (rows 9, 10, 12).** The wave graphs and the stationary-wave diagrams are drawn by the engine from the same
  numbers as the answers (waveFig, harmonicFig), so a pupil reads the figure, not a typed value.
