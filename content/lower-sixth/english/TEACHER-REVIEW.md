# Lower Sixth English — teacher review

- TR-english-1: Confirm Term One scope and progression against the school’s Advanced English course. Row 1 diagnostic and row 16 explicit review are excluded even though the parser labels every row lesson. No official papers, weighting, word limits or marking scheme are inferred from the diagnostic title.
- TR-english-2: All comprehension, prose, drama and creative models are original classroom material. They are not replacements for prescribed books. Confirm depth of narrator reliability, character, setting, conflict and stage interpretation before classroom approval.
- TR-english-3: Row 8 supplies written sound-symbol preparation using an explicit British dictionary teaching model. Local and other English varieties differ. Teachers must demonstrate and assess actual hearing, articulation, stress and interaction separately. The app does not claim pronunciation mastery from a symbol choice.
- TR-english-4: Row 19 uses the narrow shared-spelling-and-pronunciation homonym convention, while some courses use homonym more broadly. Confirm the local examination convention and accent assumptions for homophones. Do not treat differing dialect pronunciation as absence of communicative ability.
- TR-english-5: Rows 9/11/17/22/23 explicitly extend validated Form Five foundation banks with new goals, connections and a Lower Sixth comparison/scope model. Confirm whether additional course-specific difficulty or genres are required. Writing uses paper prompts/models/checklists, not automatic judgement of originality or quality.
- TR-english-6: Confirm preferred formal-letter layouts, salutation/closing conventions, essay terminology and acceptable alternative structures. The inherited letter pair is a foundation model; teachers should give a fresh task and assess purpose, register and support rather than require memorised wording.
- TR-english-7: Grammar choices target stated edited patterns. Countability, reference, comparison and word class are contextual; some sentences admit other forms when their meanings differ. Confirm accepted alternative repairs and language-variety policy. Malapropism is distinguished from every spelling error.
- TR-english-8: Summary, paraphrase, inference and retelling retain quantifiers, certainty, chronology and causation. Invented trials do not establish real outcomes. Teachers should add authentic texts, acknowledgement conventions and extended evidence questions. Technical checks do not establish exam readiness or actual learning.
