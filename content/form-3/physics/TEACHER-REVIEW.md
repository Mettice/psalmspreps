# Form 3 Physics: points for the subject teacher (TR-P3-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Open items:

- **TR-P3-1.** g = 10 N/kg throughout, as in Forms 1 and 2 (TR-P03). On the Moon, g = 1.6 N/kg.
- **TR-P3-2 (rows 1–6).** Prefixes taught: M, k, c, m, µ. Standard form a × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ a < 10. The kelvin conversion uses + 273.
  A pendulum period is found as the mean of three timings, divided by the number of swings.
- **TR-P3-3 (rows 7–9).** Typical densities: cork 0.25, wood 0.6, palm oil 0.9, water 1, aluminium 2.7, iron/steel 7.9, mercury 13.6 g/cm³;
  concrete 2400 kg/m³; sea water 1030 kg/m³.
- **TR-P3-4 (rows 10–13).** Atmospheric pressure is about 100 kPa; the mercury barometer reads 760 mm, and 13 600 × 10 × 0.76 = 103 360 Pa.
  Water in a pressure cooker boils at about 120 °C; near the top of Mount Cameroon it boils at about 90 °C. These are approximate values written by hand. Please check them.
- **TR-P3-5 (rows 14–15).** Hooke's law F = ke. The limit of proportionality and the elastic limit are both named, but not told apart in detail.
- **TR-P3-6 (rows 16–17).** The Cameroon dams named are Edéa, Song Loulou, Lagdo and Lom Pangar. Efficiency is useful ÷ input × 100 %.
  The free-fall speed uses v² = 2gh, with air resistance ignored.
- **TR-P3-7 (rows 2–3, 8, 13).** The ordering questions (density method, boiling at altitude) have one fixed correct order, written by hand.
