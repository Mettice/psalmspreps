# Form Three Logic — Term One

Draft lesson rows: 1–4 and 6–9. Guided-work row 5 is not shipped as a separate lesson.
Every lesson has Learn screens and five practice questions. Content variation is checked independently of option order.

- [ ] **TR-logic-1 — Traditional terminology.** Confirm the preferred local syllabus meanings of formal/material logic, divisions/types of logic, apprehension/judgment/reasoning, and intension/comprehension/extension. The lessons explicitly distinguish classification schemes rather than identifying them with one another.
- [ ] **TR-logic-2 — Classical framework.** Check the explanations of identity, non-contradiction and excluded middle, including same time/respect and unknown versus false. Other systems are acknowledged; no claim is made that every logic uses the classical framework.
- [ ] **TR-logic-3 — Argument assessment.** Check validity versus premise truth, induction's probable support, relevance and the original Cameroonian classroom examples. Examples framed as hypothetical are not historical or empirical evidence.
- [ ] **TR-logic-4 — Computed truth values.** Row 6 computes the conjunction of each possible pair of Boolean inputs. Confirm that truth-value notation and task difficulty fit this stage of the course.

Source summaries from OpenStax and the Stanford Encyclopedia were consulted on 2026-10-23. A local Logic teacher has not reviewed or approved these drafts.
