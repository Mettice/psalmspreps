# Form Three Geography — Term One

Taught rows: 1, 3–17. Rows 4, 6 and 8 are guided practical preparation. Further-study row 2 is not a separate lesson. Each taught row has Learn and five practice questions.

- [ ] **TR-geography-1 — Coordinates and ideal time.** Check latitude-first notation, invented graticules, local solar time, sign conventions and date carry. Exercises use ideal meridians, not current civil time-zone rules or actual coordinates of named towns.
- [ ] **TR-geography-2 — Earth models.** Check the approximate mean radius and equatorial diameter, near-spherical shape, rotation/revolution, tilt and daylight explanation. Mean and equatorial measures are labelled separately. The yearly orbit is not presented as a complete explanation of local rainfall seasons.
- [ ] **TR-geography-3 — Geology and regional interpretation.** Verify rock classification, continental-drift evidence and limitations, boundary processes and intrusive/extrusive features. The Cameroon Volcanic Line is not assumed to be a plate boundary. Cross-sections are schematic, not to scale.
- [ ] **TR-geography-4 — Hazard education.** Check age-appropriate earthquake protection, volcanic risk, exposure and preparedness. Official USGS safety summaries were consulted, but a school must adapt practice to its buildings and local authority advice. No event prediction, active-vent visit or fieldwork is assigned.

NASA/NOAA/USGS educational summaries consulted 2026-10-23; full USGS Dynamic Earth pages could not be fetched (403). Regional syllabus terminology and classroom suitability await teacher approval.
