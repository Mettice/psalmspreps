# Form Three Economics — Term One

Rows 1–14, including guided practical preparation at rows 5, 6 and 8. Each lesson has Learn screens and five questions. Amounts, utility differences and chart readings are computed. All datasets are hypothetical classroom examples.

- [ ] **TR-economics-1 — Scope of the broad spine titles.** Confirm that fundamentals cover scarcity, preferences and opportunity cost, and tools cover variables, tables, axes and scales at the intended depth for Form Three.
- [ ] **TR-economics-2 — Utility convention.** Utility units are explicitly assumed and subjective. The lessons distinguish diminishing marginal utility from decreasing total utility and do not claim objective comparisons of different people's satisfaction.
- [ ] **TR-economics-3 — Systems and market limits.** Confirm the treatment of ownership versus coordination, public provision versus public goods, third-party effects, market power and inequality. Current country-specific policy claims are deliberately avoided.
- [ ] **TR-economics-4 — Practical preparation.** Check the bar/pie diagrams, scale interpretation, hypothetical utility tables and computed monetary balances/profit. No physical experiment, actual market survey or investment recommendation is involved.

OpenStax reference summaries were consulted for introductory scarcity/choice terminology on 2026-10-23. A Cameroonian Economics teacher has not approved these drafts.
