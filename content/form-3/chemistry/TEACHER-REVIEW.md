# Form 3 Chemistry: points for the subject teacher (TR-K3-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Open items:

- **TR-K3-1.** Rows 8, 14 and 27 (integration activities) are not written as lessons.
- **TR-K3-2 (rows 1–7).** Element names come from the shared element table, which spells "sulfur". The text written for these lessons says "sulphur"; please say which spelling the school uses.
  RAMs are whole numbers (Cl = 35 in the table, 35.5 where isotopes are discussed). The Avogadro constant is 6.02 × 10²³.
  The natural abundances used (Cl 75/25, Cu 69/31, B 20/80, Ne 90/10, Br 50/50) are rounded textbook values.
- **TR-K3-3 (rows 9–13).** Electron configurations use the 2, 8, 8 rule for the first 20 elements. Group VIII is also called group 0.
  The reactions with water and the trends down the groups are written by hand.
- **TR-K3-4 (rows 15–26).** Chemical equations and formulae with digits (e.g. 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂) are written as fixed text, not computed.
  Please check each one. The VSEPR bond angles are CH₄ 109.5°, NH₃ 107°, H₂O 104.5°, CO₂ 180° and BF₃ 120°.
  BF₃ and BeCl₂ are standard VSEPR examples, but they are exceptions to the octet rule.
- **TR-K3-5.** The ordering questions (diffusion ring, forming NaCl) have one fixed correct order.
