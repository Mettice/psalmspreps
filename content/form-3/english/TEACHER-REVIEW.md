# Form Three English — Term One

Draft teaching rows 2–39; introductory diagnostic row 1 excluded. All have Learn, five templates and at least three actual content variants. Writing lessons have original paper tasks, model answers and self-checks.

- [ ] **TR-english-1 — Syllabus and examination formats.** Check paragraph length, letter layouts, schedules, articles, cheque/payment forms and the expected number-word style. Models are illustrative, not the only accepted answers. Monetary examples are fictional.
- [ ] **TR-english-2 — Oral scope.** Speaking/listening rows provide written dialogues and preparation only. Rows 8 and 16 group written IPA examples; they do not assess hearing or pronunciation. Confirm the class's pronunciation model, including GOAT vowel variation, and supply recordings or teacher-led practice.
- [ ] **TR-english-3 — Sensitive topics.** Review rows 9, 11, 36 and 39 for class suitability. Health discussions use fictional cases, respectful wording, no required disclosure and appropriate trained support. They do not diagnose, prescribe treatment or state Cameroonian legal rules.
- [ ] **TR-english-4 — Language flexibility.** Accept standard alternatives in adverb position, far comparisons, future expressions, punctuation and writing style when context supports them. Models should not become artificially exclusive marking schemes.
- [ ] **TR-english-5 — Contextual facts.** Career entry requirements, banking conditions, fares, crop prices and household practices are deliberately not presented as current universal facts. Confirm any real examples before adding them. Food writing requires an adult-approved recipe; the lesson supplies no invented cooking temperatures.

Original dialogues/passages are not prescribed literature. These drafts still need a Cameroon English teacher's review for exam depth and conventions.
