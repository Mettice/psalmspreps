# Form 3 Accounting: points for the subject teacher (TR-A3-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Open items:

- **TR-A3-1.** Row 9 (integration activities) is not written as a lesson. The spine numbers the trade-bills lesson as lesson 9 (row 10); the app files it as lesson 10.
- **TR-A3-2 (all rows).** Account numbers follow the SYSCOHADA chart as we understand it. Please check each one:
  101 Capital, 162 Loans, 231 Buildings, 241 Equipment, 244 Office furniture and equipment, 245 Transport equipment, 2183 Computer equipment,
  401 Suppliers, 402 Suppliers, bills payable, 4094 Suppliers, deposits on containers, 411 Customers, 412 Customers, bills receivable,
  4194 Customers, consigned containers, 443 VAT invoiced, 445 VAT recoverable (4451 on fixed assets), 4441 VAT payable, 4449 VAT credit,
  481 Suppliers of fixed assets, 521 Bank, 571 Cash, 585 Internal transfers, 601 Purchases of goods, 661 Salaries, 671 Interest,
  673 Discounts granted, 701 Sales of goods.
- **TR-A3-3 (rows 3, 5, 6).** VAT is 19.25% (the 17.5% rate plus the 10% council surcharge on it). Amounts are chosen so the VAT comes out in whole francs.
  Purchases use 445 for VAT recoverable; fixed assets use 4451. Does the class use the four-digit sub-accounts (4452 and so on) for purchases too?
- **TR-A3-4 (row 7).** Container deposits carry no VAT. Containers the customer keeps are treated as sold. The lessons do not give that sale's entry or its VAT.
- **TR-A3-5 (row 8).** The cash-discount questions take the discount off the amount owed. They do not adjust the VAT on the discount (a Form 3 simplification).
  The bounced-cheque item reverses the receipt: Dr 411 / Cr 521.
- **TR-A3-6 (row 10).** Trade bills: seller Dr 412 / Cr 411, then Dr 521 / Cr 412 at maturity; buyer Dr 401 / Cr 402, then Dr 402 / Cr 521.
  Discounting is mentioned but has no entries. The due-date question counts days from 1 March in a non-leap year (1 March = day 60).
