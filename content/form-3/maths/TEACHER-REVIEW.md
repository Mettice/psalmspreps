# Form 3 Maths: points for the subject teacher (TR-M3-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Please check the mathematics, the level for Form 3,
and the wording. Open items:

- **TR-M3-1 (row 1).** "Contact with students and Diagnostic Assessment" is not written as a lesson.
- **TR-M3-2 (rows 2–7).** Rows 2 and 3 revise Form 2 algebra before the new work: like terms with powers, products and
  quotients of terms, brackets with a minus sign. Row 7 covers equations with fractions in the form (x + a) ÷ m = (x − b) ÷ n,
  written with ÷ because the app shows a fraction on one line. Is that acceptable, or should these wait for a fraction display?
- **TR-M3-3 (row 5).** Trinomials with a ≠ 1 are factorised by trial (factor pairs), not the "ac method". Which
  method does the school teach?
- **TR-M3-4 (rows 8–9).** Answers to simultaneous equations are written "x = 3, y = −1". Pupils type single values (x or y)
  in some questions and choose the pair in others.
- **TR-M3-5 (row 10).** Contexts: ages, a garden in Buea, a classroom floor, prices in FCFA at Mile 17 market. Pen and
  book prices are multiples of 50 FCFA. Are they realistic?
- **TR-M3-6 (row 12).** The quadratic formula is shown as x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. Roots are given to 2 decimal places;
  the app accepts −1.6 and −1.60 as the same answer. The discriminant decides the number of roots (two, one repeated, none).
