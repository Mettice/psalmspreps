# Form 3 Maths: points for the subject teacher (TR-M3-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Please check the mathematics, the level for Form 3,
and the wording. Open items:

- **TR-M3-1 (row 1).** "Contact with students and Diagnostic Assessment" is not written as a lesson.
- **TR-M3-2 (rows 2–7).** Rows 2 and 3 revise Form 2 algebra before the new work: like terms with powers, products and
  quotients of terms, brackets with a minus sign. Row 7 covers equations with fractions in the form (x + a) ÷ m = (x − b) ÷ n,
  written with ÷ because the app shows a fraction on one line. Is that acceptable, or should these wait for a fraction display?
- **TR-M3-3 (row 5).** Trinomials with a ≠ 1 are factorised by trial (factor pairs), not the "ac method". Which
  method does the school teach?
- **TR-M3-4 (rows 8–9).** Answers to simultaneous equations are written "x = 3, y = −1". Pupils type single values (x or y)
  in some questions and choose the pair in others.
- **TR-M3-5 (row 10).** Contexts: ages, a garden in Buea, a classroom floor, prices in FCFA at Mile 17 market. Pen and
  book prices are multiples of 50 FCFA. Are they realistic?
- **TR-M3-6 (row 12).** The quadratic formula is shown as x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. Roots are given to 2 decimal places;
  the app accepts −1.6 and −1.60 as the same answer. The discriminant decides the number of roots (two, one repeated, none).
- **TR-M3-7 (row 13).** The rearranged formulae (v = u + at, P = 2l + 2w, y = mx + c, A = ½(a + b)h, A = πr², E = ½mv²,
  ax + b = cx + d, y = (x + 1) ÷ (x − 1), p = (a + b) ÷ a) and the explanation of each wrong option were written by hand. Please check them.
- **TR-M3-8 (rows 14–15).** Variation is written as y = kx, y = k ÷ x, z = kxy, z = kx ÷ y. Partial variation (y = a + bx), listed in some
  textbooks, is not covered yet. Should it be?
- **TR-M3-9 (rows 16–19).** Angles are in degrees and the calculator is assumed to be in degree mode. Exact values: tan 30° = 1/√3 and
  sin 45° = 1/√2; the lesson also mentions the rationalised form √2/2. Sides and angles are given to 1 decimal place, distances to the
  nearest metre. The angle of depression is explained as equal to the angle of elevation (alternate angles).
- **TR-M3-10 (rows 20–24).** In option lists a matrix is written on one line with semicolons between the rows, (2 3 ; 1 4); questions and
  examples also draw the matrix with brackets. The inverse of a 2 × 2 matrix is written (1/|A|) × adj A. Inverse matrices are used to
  solve simultaneous equations only where |A| = 1. Should other determinants be used too?
- **TR-M3-11 (rows 25–26).** The congruence tests (SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS) and the proof reasons are written by hand. SSA and AAA are
  taught as "not tests".
- **TR-M3-12 (rows 27–30).** Thales is taught as AD/AB = AE/AC = DE/BC and AD/DB = AE/EC, with its converse. The contexts (map of a farm
  near Kumba, river width, groundnut-oil tins priced in FCFA, minibus model) need a quick check that they are realistic.
