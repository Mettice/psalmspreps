# Form 3 Biology: points for the subject teacher (TR-B3-n)

Every lesson, Learn screen, card and worked example is **draft**. Open items:

- **TR-B3-1.** Rows 10–12 and 22–24 (integration, evaluation, remediation) are not written as lessons.
- **TR-B3-2 (rows 3 and 8, practicals).** These are taught as guided preparation: method in order, stains, drawing rules and
  calculations. The pupil does not do the practical in the app. Please say whether this is enough for the school.
- **TR-B3-3 (rows 1–2).** Size ranges: prokaryotes 1–10 µm, eukaryotic cells 10–100 µm. Organelles: nucleus, mitochondria,
  ribosomes, ER, Golgi body, centrioles. The lettered plant-cell drawing shows six parts.
- **TR-B3-4 (rows 4–8).** Body water is taken as 60–70 %. Osmosis is written as "water moves from dilute to concentrated solution",
  rather than in terms of water potential. Animal cells in water "burst" (haemolysis); in strong solutions they are "crenated".
- **TR-B3-5 (rows 13–21).** The facts written by hand need checking: T2 structure and replication; bacterial shapes; the life
  cycle of Plasmodium (fever every 48–72 hours); malaria control; fungi (chitin walls); monocot and dicot differences; tilapia's
  parts and adaptations; Cameroon examples (Lagdo and Mape dams, black pod of cocoa, palm wine fermentation).
  Bacteria are said to divide every 20–30 minutes in good conditions.
